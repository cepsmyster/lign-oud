01The wood

A tree makes this only when it is hurt.

Healthy Aquilaria wood is pale, light and has no smell. Wounded and infected, the tree answers with a dark resin. Years later, that resin is agarwood.

We buy the grade that sinks in water. Then we burn a chip from every lot before we distil it. If the smoke is dull, the lot goes back.

02Three forests

Three places, kept apart.

  1. Nahor Bari

    Aquilaria malaccensis120 mCut November to January

    River plain and home gardens. Sweet hay, leather, a barn in winter.

  2. Phu Mok

    Aquilaria crassna640 mCut January to March

    Hill forest on red soil. Stewed plum, honey, pipe tobacco.

  3. Batu Rimba

    Aquilaria beccariana310 mCut June to August

    Lowland rain forest. Green, camphor, wet stone.

Never blended. A batch is one place and one season.

Batch 07, on skin.

One drop is a dose. Use the rod, not the bottle.
